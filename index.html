Help & support

Learn more about the online fault process

How do I track the progress of a fault?
You can track your fault anytime from this page. We'll update the progress tracker as your fault moves through each stage, and notify you when action is needed.
What does 'Action needed' mean?
'Action needed' means we need something from you before we can continue — for example, finishing your fault report or confirming an appointment.
What happens if my service still isn't working but the fault has been closed?
If your service still isn't working after a fault is closed, you can reopen it or report a new fault and we'll investigate again.
